## Getting to the Venue

For airport connections, rail travel and local bus services, see [Travel & Transportation]({{ '/attending/transportation/' | relative_url }}).

### From Lancaster Railway Station

Arrive at **Lancaster railway station** for the city centre. From the station, you can walk or take a taxi to Lancaster Town Hall in Dalton Square. Use the [walking directions from the station](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Lancaster+Railway+Station+Lancaster+UK&destination=Lancaster+Town+Hall+Dalton+Square+Lancaster+LA1+1PJ+UK&travelmode=walking) to plan your route, and [National Rail](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/) to check train services.

### From Lancaster University Campus

The conference venue is in **Lancaster city centre**. If you are staying at the University's Bailrigg Campus, including the Guest Rooms, please allow time to travel to the Town Hall by bus or taxi. See [Lancaster University's maps and travel information](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/about-us/maps-and-travel/) for routes between campus and the city centre.

### By Car / Uber / Taxi

Use **LA1 1PJ** to locate the Town Hall. For public parking locations and current charges, consult [Lancaster City Council's car park information](https://lancaster.gov.uk/parking/car-parks).
