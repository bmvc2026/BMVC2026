## By Air

### Manchester Airport (MAN)

1. After arrival, follow signs to **Manchester Airport railway station**. The station is linked to the terminals by walkways; see the [airport's train travel guide](https://www.manchesterairport.co.uk/getting-to-and-from/by-train/) for directions.
2. Search for a train from **Manchester Airport** to **Lancaster**. Direct services are available, while other departures require a change. Check the [Manchester Airport to Lancaster route](https://www.tpexpress.co.uk/travelling-with-us/routes/trains-from-manchester-airport-to-lancaster) or [National Rail](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/) for the journey time, changes and fares on your travel date.
3. From Lancaster station, continue to the Town Hall on foot or by taxi, as described below.

Allow time between your flight and train for passport control, baggage collection and the walk to the station. If your flight arrives late in the evening, check the last onward train before booking your journey.

### London Heathrow (LHR) and London Gatwick (LGW)

If you arrive at a London airport, travel into central London and transfer to **London Euston** for a train to Lancaster. Possible routes include:

- **Heathrow:** take the Heathrow Express to Paddington, then use the London Underground to reach Euston.
- **Gatwick:** take the Gatwick Express to Victoria, then use the London Underground to reach Euston.

These routes are also described in [Lancaster University's travel guide](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/about-us/maps-and-travel/). Use the [Transport for London journey planner](https://tfl.gov.uk/plan-a-journey/) for the transfer within London and [Avanti West Coast's London Euston to Lancaster page](https://www.avantiwestcoast.co.uk/travel-information/train-times/london-euston/lancaster) for onward trains. Allow additional time for the airport transfer and connections.

## By Train

Book your rail journey to **Lancaster (LAN)**. The station is on **Meeting House Lane, Lancaster, LA1 5NW**. The [National Rail station guide](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/stations/lancaster/) provides live departures, facilities, accessibility information and onward travel details.

Direct services from **London Euston** are available with [Avanti West Coast](https://www.avantiwestcoast.co.uk/travel-information/train-times/london-euston/lancaster). For journeys from other UK cities, use [National Rail](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/) to compare routes and connections.

When booking, check which trains your ticket permits you to use. Recheck your journey before departure for timetable changes and engineering work.

## From Lancaster Station to the Venue

The destination is **Lancaster Town Hall, Dalton Square, LA1 1PJ**.

- **On foot:** follow the [walking route from Lancaster railway station to the Town Hall](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Lancaster+Railway+Station+Lancaster+UK&destination=Lancaster+Town+Hall+Dalton+Square+Lancaster+LA1+1PJ+UK&travelmode=walking).
- **By taxi:** ask the driver for Lancaster Town Hall in Dalton Square. This may be convenient if you are travelling with luggage; check the [station's onward travel information](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/stations/lancaster/) for transport facilities.

The [Venue page]({{ '/attending/venue/' | relative_url }}) includes a map and a photograph of the building to help you identify it on arrival.

## Local Transport and University Accommodation

### Between Bailrigg Campus and the City Centre

Lancaster University's main campus is approximately **three miles south of the city centre**. If you are staying in the [University Guest Rooms](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/conferences/guest-rooms/), you will need to travel into the city for the conference.

Stagecoach services **1 and 1A** connect the campus and city centre. **Lancaster University Underpass**, beneath Alexandra Square, is a useful campus stop, and **Common Garden Street** is a city-centre stop from which you can continue on foot to Dalton Square. Check the direction and stops of your chosen service before boarding.

Use [Lancaster University's bus and travel guide](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/about-us/maps-and-travel/) and the [Stagecoach journey planner](https://www.stagecoachbus.com/) for current services, fares and return journeys. You can also [plan a public transport journey from campus to the Town Hall](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Lancaster+University+Underpass+Lancaster+UK&destination=Lancaster+Town+Hall+Dalton+Square+Lancaster+LA1+1PJ+UK&travelmode=transit).

### Taxis

A taxi can take you between your accommodation, the station and the venue. Give the full destination address when booking and ask the operator to confirm the fare. Lancaster University's [public transport page](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/sustainability/action/travel/public-transport/) lists local taxi operators.

## Driving & Parking

For the conference venue, use **Lancaster Town Hall, LA1 1PJ** in your route planner. Consult [Lancaster City Council's car park information](https://lancaster.gov.uk/parking/car-parks) for city-centre parking locations and current charges.
