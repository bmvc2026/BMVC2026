## Lancaster University Guest Rooms

[Lancaster University Guest Rooms](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/conferences/guest-rooms/) offer single, twin and double en-suite accommodation on the University's **Bailrigg Campus**. Visit the University's website for room details, current prices and the booking link, and check availability for your intended dates.

The campus is approximately **three miles south of Lancaster city centre**. If you choose this accommodation, allow time to travel to Lancaster Town Hall by bus or taxi each day. See [Travel & Transportation]({{ '/attending/transportation/' | relative_url }}#between-bailrigg-campus-and-the-city-centre) for campus connections.

The Guest Rooms building has no lift. If you require a ground-floor room or have other access requirements, contact the Guest Rooms team before booking using the details on the [University's contact page](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/conferences/contact-us/).

## Booking Information

**BMVC {{ site.conf_year }} has not arranged special accommodation rates or reserved rooms for attendees.** Please book directly with your chosen accommodation provider. Prices and availability are subject to change.

Before confirming your booking, check the cancellation terms and whether breakfast and any parking you need are included.
