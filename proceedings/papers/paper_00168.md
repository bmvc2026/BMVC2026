title: Robust Neural Surface Reconstruction without Geometric Perturbation

- Jiyoon Shin
- Sangwoo Hong

abstract: |-
  Unsupervised neural reconstruction from sparse and noisy point clouds is challenging due to uncertainty in pseudo-labels induced by corrupted observations.
  Direct supervision with such inputs often leads to excessive smoothing in early training and overfitting to noise in later stages.
  This failure is fundamentally statistical rather than geometric: while geometric consistency must be preserved, the primary difficulty lies in uncertainty within the pseudo-label distribution.
  Accordingly, unsupervised neural reconstruction is formulated as a distributionally robust optimization (DRO) problem that models uncertainty at the distribution level without altering spatial geometry.
  An uncertainty set defined by Rényi divergence leads to a Rényi-DRO framework that preserves geometry by reweighting samples instead of relocating query points, naturally aligning with Neural Pull’s projection-based enforcement.
  By leveraging duality, the worst-case distribution admits a closed-form solution via exponential tilting, avoiding inner adversarial optimization.
  Extensive experiments across multiple datasets under severe sparsity and noise demonstrate consistent improvements in reconstruction quality and robustness.
