abstract: |-
  Exposure correction aims to recover well-exposed images from under- or over-exposed inputs.
  Most existing methods directly learn an RGB-to-RGB mapping without explicitly separating exposure-invariant scene properties from exposure-dependent appearance.
  We revisit exposure correction from the perspective of intrinsic image decomposition and propose Exposure-Aware Intrinsic Image Decomposition (EA-IID), which explicitly separates exposure-invariant albedo from exposure-dependent shading.
  EA-IID enforces consistent albedo across different exposures of the same scene and calibrates the corresponding shading to reconstruct a canonical exposure.
  Since ground-truth albedo is unavailable for real scenes, we use pseudo-albedo estimates from a pretrained intrinsic decomposition model with uncertainty-aware supervision, together with cross-exposure consistency regularization.
  Experiments on intrinsic decomposition and exposure-correction benchmarks show that EA-IID produces more consistent albedo across exposure levels while achieving state-of-the-art exposure-correction performance.
  The project page is available at https://hyunjun-koh.github.io/EA-IID/.
