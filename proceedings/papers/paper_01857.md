abstract: |-
  Thin-structure segmentation---retinal vessels, pavement cracks,
  neurites---demands sub-pixel fidelity yet must run within a tight compute
  budget.  We trace the failure of standard encoder--decoders to a single
  cause: they allocate compute \emph{uniformly} across scales, whereas the
  information geometry of thin structures is acutely \emph{non-uniform}.
  Foreground occupies ${\le}5\%$ of pixels, discriminative evidence collapses
  into narrow frequency bands, and a single mislabelled pixel can sever an
  entire connected component---so uniformly spent FLOPs carry almost no
  signal.  We therefore recast architecture design as \emph{compute
  allocation} and report a \emph{super-additivity observation}: under an
  idealised independent-error model, jointly correcting three failure
  modes---spectral attenuation, topological fragmentation, and boundary
  erosion---empirically yields an accuracy gain that exceeds the sum of the
  individual corrections, at only \emph{additive} compute cost.
  \textbf{FTR-Net} (\textbf{F}requency--\textbf{T}opology--%
  \textbf{R}efinement Network) realises this observation through three
  stage-aware primitives, each placed where it maximises information
  recovered per FLOP: an \emph{Adaptive Frequency Gate} performs
  input-dependent, per-channel spectral fusion to restore the high-frequency
  bands that stride-2 sampling attenuates; a \emph{Topology Router}
  prioritises, in the forward pass, the junction- and endpoint-critical
  pixels that pixel-wise losses systematically neglect; and a \emph{Neural
  Cellular Automata Refiner} sharpens boundaries at full resolution under a
  provable contraction guarantee at negligible parameter cost.  At only
  $8.51$\,M parameters, FTR-Net achieves the best F1, IoU, and clDice
  across CrackForest, DRIVE, and HRF (mean F1\,$=$\,$0.760$,
  IoU\,$=$\,$0.616$, clDice\,$=$\,$0.754$) and scales to FIVES
  ($800$ images), surpassing the next-best baseline (ITENet) by
  $+3.8\%$\,F1 and $+6.0\%$\,clDice with $2.7{\times}$ fewer
  parameters---a margin consistent with matching compute to the
  task's information geometry rather than spending it uniformly.  A
  three-seed component ablation is consistent with the super-additivity
  observation: the joint gain ($+0.105\!\pm\!.010$\,F1) exceeds the sum of the
  single-term contributions ($+0.064$) by a bonus of $+0.041\!\pm\!.012$\,F1
  ($p{<}0.05$), and the disproportionate clDice improvement is consistent with
  forward-pass topological reasoning preserving connectivity that
  topology-agnostic designs do not recover as well.
