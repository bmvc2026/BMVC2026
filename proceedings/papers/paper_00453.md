title: 'RaSelect: Reinforcement-Learned View Selection for Multi-View Radar Human Pose Estimation'
authors:
- Hsi Lin
- Mariko Isogawa

abstract: |-
  Multi-view radar enables privacy-preserving, illumination-robust 3D
  human pose estimation, but its accuracy depends critically on the
  spatial arrangement of sensors. The question of which viewpoints to
  instrument is typically answered by manual heuristics or exhaustive
  placement studies; recent radar pose systems have converged on a
  two-radar layout inherited from early benchmarks, and how to extend
  this to larger arrays remains open. In the RGB-camera setting, deep
  reinforcement learning (RL) has been used to actively select small,
  informative subsets of viewpoints from larger arrays. We observe that
  this idea has not been transferred to radar sensing, despite radar
  arrays facing fundamentally the same placement question.

  We propose RaSelect, to our knowledge the first RL-based
  view-selection framework for multi-view radar human pose. RaSelect
  frames sensor selection as learned spatial optimization. A
  value-based policy is trained jointly with a late-fusion pose
  network -- per-view CNN regressors aggregated by softmax attention --
  to choose, per frame, the small subset of radar viewpoints that
  minimizes per-joint position error. Training uses a potential-based
  shaped reward on the prediction error. This yields a per-frame policy
  that adapts the active subset to the current scene, together with a
  data-driven ranking of the radar positions it relies on. On a simulated 33-viewpoint radar dataset,
  RaSelect at K=5 reduces per-joint position error by roughly a third
  compared to random K-view selection, matches naive fusion of all 33 viewpoints, and surpasses the best
  static K-view placement on all three metrics even when the latter is
  selected with oracle access to the test set. The
  learned policy is robust to
  the choice of initial viewpoint, indicating that it discovers a
  consistent informative subset rather than an arbitrary one. RaSelect thus lifts multi-view radar view selection from manual
  heuristics to learned spatial optimization.
  Code is available at https://github.com/wwzz1/RaSelect.
