abstract: Recent progress in text-to-image diffusion has increased the need for invisible and tamper-resilient watermarking that preserves visual fidelity and prompt alignment. Existing methods often trade off robustness, imperceptibility, or scalability, and may introduce semantic drift. We introduce NullGuard, a training-free, plug-and-play framework that embeds cryptographically keyed signals in low-sensitivity directions of pretrained diffusion Jacobians through user-specific rotations. A lightweight Gauss--Newton pivot with perceptual masking restricts perturbations to watermark-relevant components, while a calibrated keyed forward likelihood-gap test enables reliable verification. Experiments on MS-COCO and DiffusionDB show that NullGuard achieves up to 99\% detection under common attacks, including blur and JPEG compression, while maintaining PSNR $\ge$ 45 dB and strong semantic fidelity.
