abstract: |-
  A spherocylinder is a cylinder capped at one end by a hemisphere of equal radius.
  We present $\texttt{ScyPoSe}$ ($\texttt{S}$phero$\texttt{cy}$linder $\texttt{Po}$se from $\texttt{S}$ilhouett$\texttt{e}$), a theory and method for estimating the spherocylinder $5$-DoF pose from its silhouette in a single 2D image.
  $\texttt{ScyPoSe}$ exploits the geometric property that the spherically-normalised silhouette of a spherocylinder is formed of circular arcs on the camera-centred unit sphere: two great-circle arcs from the cylinder  and a single small-circle arc from the hemisphere. This structure reduces pose estimation to a sequence of constrained plane fitting problems, admitting simple, fast, and convex solutions for both the minimal $5$-point and redundant $n>5$ point cases. We further introduce $\texttt{Robust-ScyPoSe}$, a RANSAC-based extension that jointly estimates pose, classifies silhouette points, and rejects erroneous ones. Experiments on synthetic and real data demonstrate that $\texttt{Robust-ScyPoSe}$ pushes the state of the art forward in accuracy and robustness.
