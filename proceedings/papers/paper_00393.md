abstract: |-
  Cast shadows introduce spatially non-uniform luminance attenuation, colour shifts, and texture suppression that degrade perceptual quality and downstream vision reliability. We present SAGNet, a compact encoder-decoder for image shadow removal that internally
  estimates a coherent shadow mask without any external annotation. Our method integrates four tightly coupled modules: a Mask-Guided
  Pathway (MGP) combining cascade mask estimation and Shadow-Aware Feature Modulation (SAFM) that decomposes features into shadow and non-shadow pathways conditioned on the internally estimated mask; a Light Convolutional Block (LCB) with depthwise separable convolutions and Cross-Scale Attention Fusion (CSAF) for multi-scale boundary modelling; and Uncertainty-Guided Progressive Refinement (UGPR) for spatially coherent reconstruction. At only 67.19 GFLOPs, our method achieves state-of-the-art shadow-region performance on AISTD and SRD, and the best structural fidelity on WSRD+, while running at 20.73 FPS, without any mask supervision, task-specific prompts, or auxiliary modules. The code and pre-trained models are available at https://github.com/Midrees330/SAGNet.
