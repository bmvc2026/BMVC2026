abstract: |-
  Multi-Teacher Knowledge Distillation (MTKD) distils knowledge from multiple teachers into a single compact student, making it well-suited for resource-limited deployment. Image Forgery Detection and Localisation (IFDL) exemplifies this deployment because different forgery families (e.g., Photoshop, DeepFake, and AI-Generated Content) typically require different teachers to detect and localise them, yet deploying multiple models is impractical. Existing MTKD methods assign each teacher one weight per image, which fails to exclude unreliable teachers, apply a different weight to each teacher at each pixel, or handle the imbalance between forged and authentic pixels.
  In this paper, we propose SFA-MTKD, a Spatial-Frequency-Aware Multi-Teacher Knowledge Distillation framework based on three key ideas. Tri-State Selective Refinement (TSR) selectively excludes or refines unreliable teachers. Spatial-Frequency-Aware Pixel Routing (SFAPR) assigns each teacher a weight at every pixel by analysing the image's frequency and entropy information. Forgery-Aware Focal Distillation (FFD) upweights minority forged pixels by assigning them a larger focal exponent than authentic pixels. SFA-MTKD improves average localisation pixel-level IoU by 29% over the strongest MTKD baseline across three forgery families with a compact ResNet-18 student. Ablation studies confirm that all three ideas contribute complementary gains.
