abstract: |-
  Multi-modal 3D object detection based on camera-LiDAR fusion is essential for reliable autonomous driving. However, real-world sensor systems are susceptible to diverse degradations such as noise, adverse weather, and sensor dropout, which can severely compromise detection performance. Representative query-based multi-modal methods exhibit an asymmetry in encoder-side spatial feature aggregation: LiDAR features are spatially organized in Bird’s Eye View (BEV), whereas multi-view camera features are often processed without explicit cross-view token interaction before decoding. Consequently, the decoder must perform cross-modal aggregation while also handling spatial relationships across camera views.
  We propose DUIS, a symmetric architecture that addresses this asymmetry by separating encoder-side spatial token mixing from query-conditioned local cross-modal aggregation in the decoder. At the encoder stage, View Unified Attention (VUA) applies self-attention to position-aware image tokens from multiple camera views, enabling geometry-aware cross-view token interaction before decoding. At the decoder stage, Modality Unified Deformable Attention (MUDA) projects shared 3D sampling points around each query anchor onto both the image and BEV feature planes, enabling local feature aggregation from both modalities. Experiments on the MultiCorrupt benchmark demonstrate that DUIS achieves strong overall robustness across 10 sensor corruption types at 3 severity levels, achieving a mean mAP of 0.566 and NDS of 0.637.
