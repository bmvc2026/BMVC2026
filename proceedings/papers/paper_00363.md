abstract: Acoustic-based 3D human pose estimation relies on reflected sound signals, where rich multipath propagation entangles pose information with environment-dependent reflections, often dominating pose cues and limiting current methods to single-environment settings. In this work, we introduce a cross-environment acoustic dataset and present a pose-aware weighted contrastive learning framework (PAW-CL) that addresses environment-induced domain shift through sample-level alignment across environments via pose similarity, with adaptive weighting to account for continuous pose structure. Experimental results show that the proposed framework improves generalization to unseen environments, reducing RMSE and MAE by 29.3\% and 30.5\% (relative) and improving PCK@0.2 from 0.28 to 0.42 compared to the same backbone without PAW-CL, while better capturing motion details under environment changes.
